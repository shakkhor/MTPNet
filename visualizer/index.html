    Parameters

    Values are on the rescaled scale the model is trained on (each variable has mean 0 and a typical swing of 1 on the training data), so misses compare across variables. "Badly wrong" means off by more than 1.0. Trend is a one-day moving average, seasonal is the average shape at each time of day within a forecast, local is what is left. Long ranges lay each run's forecasts end to end, one per prediction length.
